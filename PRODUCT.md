## What this is

**Maintain Worker** is Australia's B2B **workforce exchange** for construction —
a Maintain / The Pep Collective venture, sister product to QuoteMax. Construction
companies share trusted, skilled labour with one another, directly, without a
labour hire middleman. One side lists **available workforce**, the other posts a
**labour need**; every introduction in v1 is facilitated manually by our team.
Trust is engineered in through **ABN verification**: the network is closed and
business-to-business.

The category line: *QuoteMax helps companies win work. Maintain Worker helps
them deliver it.*

**What it is NOT (strategic, non-negotiable):** not a recruitment agency, not a
labour hire company, not a job board, not a gig marketplace. The unit of the
marketplace is the **company**, never the individual worker. The site must never
model, address, or recruit individual workers.

## Users

One audience, two postures. Construction subcontractors and builders (~5–150
employees); the buyer is an owner, operations manager, or site/project manager
who controls crew.

- **"Need Labour"** — won work beyond current crew capacity.
- **"Have Labour Available"** — project ending, crew about to be idle.

The same company cycles between both, often in the same month. They value trust,
speed, keeping the relationship, and avoiding the labour-hire margin.

## Pilot scope (v1)

Four trades — **Roofing · Plumbing · Carpentry · Electrical** (mono codes
`ROOF / PLUMB / CARP / ELEC`) — across **South East Queensland**. Civil and
commercial construction follow. The pilot is free; monetisation waits for proven
liquidity. v1 is a concierge marketplace: manual, human-reviewed introductions.

## Primary conversion

**Register your company.** The critical path is Register → Verify → List one
thing → Discover a counterpart → Request introduction, target under five
minutes. Every public page drives toward register-and-list. Secondary action:
talk to the team.

## Voice

Executive, plain, operational. Active voice, sentence case. Buttons name the
exact outcome ("Request introduction" → "Introduction requested"). No startup
hype, no apologetic microcopy, no cleverness where clarity will do. The site
reads like an operations tool that happens to have a front door.

## Honesty rules

- Every roadmap item (QuoteMax integration, messaging, automated matching) is
  labelled as roadmap, never implied as shipped.
- The company workspace (login/dashboard) is in build; the site says so plainly.
- Sample marketplace data is labelled as illustrative until the live board ships.
- Facts that must be true (counts, dates, legal entities, pricing) stay
  [bracketed] until the business confirms them.
- Labour hire licensing is under legal review (blueprint §19.1); the site must
  not make claims that prejudge that advice.

## Anti-references

Never playful, never overly corporate, never generic SaaS, never a brochure.
Banned: marketing-gradient hero with a big stat number, fake testimonials,
invented metrics, worker-facing recruitment copy.
